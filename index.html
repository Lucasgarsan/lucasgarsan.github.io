Featured Paper

Universal Cash Transfers and Income Inequality: Evidence from the Alaska Permanent Fund Dividend

Using the 1982 introduction of the Alaska Permanent Fund Dividend as a natural experiment and the synthetic control method, I find that Alaskan income inequality fell after the dividend, consistent with the policy's mechanical effect alone.
